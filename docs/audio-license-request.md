# Audio licensing — permission request (draft)

Draft email to request written permission to use quran.com word-by-word recitation audio in
Af'ham. Background and why this is needed: see [`audio-pipeline.md`](audio-pipeline.md)
(§ Licensing) — quran.com's Terms forbid redistribution without prior written consent, and a
free app does not change that.

**To:** Quran Foundation / quran.com team — via the contact form at

**Subject:** Permission request — word-by-word recitation audio in a free, non-commercial Qur'an-vocabulary app

Assalāmu ʿalaykum wa raḥmatullāh,

My name is **Nourreddine Houari**. I'm building a **free, non-commercial** mobile app called
**Af'ham (أَفْهَم)** whose purpose is to help people who read the Qur'an in Arabic but don't
understand it — bridging the vocabulary gap so reading becomes *tadabbur* rather than mechanical
recitation. The app is fully offline, bilingual (French/English), and has no ads, paid tiers, or
data collection.

I would like to request **written permission to use quran.com's word-by-word recitation audio**
(the clips served from `audio.qurancdn.com/wbw/…`) in the app. Specifically:

- For each vocabulary word, I extract the **single corresponding word clip** from the
  word-by-word audio and bundle it **offline** inside the app, played on demand when the user
  taps a word.
- I'm starting with a curated set of high-frequency words and **intend to grow the vocabulary
  substantially over time — potentially toward coverage of the full Qur'anic word-by-word audio
  set**. I'd therefore appreciate permission scoped to this ongoing expansion (not just the
  initial words), so the project can grow without re-requesting.
- I am happy to **credit Quran Foundation / quran.com prominently** (in the app's About screen
  and store listing) and to follow any attribution wording or conditions you require.

I understand from your Terms & Conditions that this content is not licensed for redistribution
without prior written consent, which is exactly why I'm reaching out before release. I want to
make sure the project respects your rights and those of the reciter.

Could you please let me know:

1. Whether you can grant permission for this **free, educational / daʿwah** use, and under what
   conditions;
2. The **reciter** of the word-by-word audio, so I can acknowledge them (and seek their
   permission too if needed);
3. Any preferred attribution text or other requirements.

The app is open-source at **github.com/nouhouari/afham** and I'm glad to share a build or more
details.

Jazākum Allāhu khayran for your work and for considering this request.

With gratitude,
**Nourreddine Houari**
[your email] · github.com/nouhouari
